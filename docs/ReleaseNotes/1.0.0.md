# DarkRoom 1.0.0

The first release of DarkRoom, a native Mac app for your Immich photo library with Lightroom-style culling and editing.

**Library**
- Multi-user sign-in with a DarkRoom PIN or Touch ID, quick switching and locking; read-only mode for a first look
- Fast grid with a date bar (click to jump, hover for the date), single-photo view with ‹ › arrows and trackpad swipes
- Search, Explore, People (hide people you don't want listed), Places, map, albums, sharing, Memories
- Info panel: click the date or location to change it; the people in each photo with their faces

**Organising**
- Ratings, favourites, pick/reject flags, tags, dates, locations, stacks, archive, locked folder, trash with undo
- Date, location and descriptions written into the files too

**Editing**
- RAW processing, light, colour, curves, colour grading, detail, effects, lens and perspective corrections
- Crop, rotate, flip, heal, masks (brush, gradients, subject, sky), presets and LUTs
- Save / Cancel / Reset, and DarkRoom always asks before leaving with unsaved changes
- Edits are saved as a version stacked on the untouched original
- HDR and panorama merge, lossless video rotation, Make Still for Live Photos
- Edit in Photoshop, Pixelmator Pro, darktable and other apps

**Import and export**
- Import from any disk straight to Immich, skipping duplicates and keeping Live Photos and RAW+JPEG pairs together
- Export with sizes, watermarks and presets; Quick Look; Shortcuts actions; menu bar status

Requires macOS 26 on Apple silicon and an Immich server.
